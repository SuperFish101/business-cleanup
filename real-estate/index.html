Straight answers

The questions people actually ask.

I've been burned by a consultant before. Why is this different?

Most consultants hand you a report, or build you something on a platform only they know how to run. When they leave, so does the value. You get plain files you own: no app, no subscription, no vendor. Written in plain English, so any person can read it and know how to do the job, and any AI can run it. If we never speak again, everything keeps working.

Is this just AI thrown at my business?

No, and I'd be suspicious of anyone selling it that way. It's 60% organizing your business, 30% fixing where deals get dropped, 10% AI on the follow-up you never get to. AI on a disorganized business just makes a mess faster. The organizing is where almost all the value is.

How much of my time does this take?

It depends on the scope. Most of your time lands early, while I'm learning how you actually work. After that it's short check-ins. I'll give you a real number once I've seen the job. The building is my job, not yours.

What if my team won't use it?

There's nothing new to log into and nothing new to learn. I'm not adding another tool on top of a CRM you already don't update. The thing they already do just gets somewhere to live and a clear order to it. For most teams it's less work, not more, which is why it sticks.

What does it cost?

Most engagements run four figures, flat. Fixed scope, one number, no hourly billing and no monthly fee. I don't quote before I've seen your business, because a solo agent and a fifteen-person team are different jobs. That's what the call is for.

What happens after the 30 days?

It's yours. You keep the system whether or not we ever work together again, plus a 90-day tune-up where I come back and fix whatever drifted, and a direct line to me for quick questions. Neither costs extra.

I'm a solo agent. Is this only for teams?

No. Solo agents are often the clearest fit, because everything genuinely lives in one head, and that head is out showing property all day. The problem isn't headcount. It's more to do than time to do it, and leads slipping as a result.
